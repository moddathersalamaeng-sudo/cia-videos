The purpose is consistent fulfilment of customer and applicable statutory and regulatory requirements, not merely a list of names.
For this trading company, start with customers, suppliers, employees and relevant regulatory bodies.
Customers define needs and delivery commitments; suppliers need clear orders; employees need suitable information and capability.
Consider regulatory bodies according to the products and activity, and add other parties where their relevance is established.
Assess a party by its actual or potential effect on the ability to fulfil quality requirements.
