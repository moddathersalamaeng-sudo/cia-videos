Clause 8.5.5 requires meeting post-delivery activity requirements associated with products and services.
Examples include warranty actions, contractual maintenance and supplementary services such as recycling or final disposal.
These examples do not mean that every organization must provide all of them.
Determine applicable product or service requirements and meet them.
A courtesy call does not replace performing an applicable obligation.
Determine the required extent considering statutory and regulatory requirements, potential undesired consequences, and product or service nature, use and intended lifetime.
Also consider customer requirements and customer feedback.
These considerations are required; do not selectively ignore them.
They do not impose identical warranty periods or follow-up visit counts for all products.
