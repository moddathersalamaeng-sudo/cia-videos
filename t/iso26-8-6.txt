A training company finishes a course; its final report looks polished and ready to send.
However, review of the attendance record required by the agreement is incomplete.
Sending it because the deadline is near does not establish that service requirements have been met.
Clause 8.6 addresses verification of requirements and authorization for customer release of products and services.
Implement planned arrangements at appropriate stages to verify that product and service requirements have been met.
Define report acceptance criteria, required reviews, timing and responsibility according to the agreement.
Criteria may include required data completeness and consistency with approved information, depending on the case.
A generic checklist cannot replace actual customer and service requirements.
Verification determines whether acceptance criteria have been met; release authorization permits the output to proceed under defined authority.
Roles vary with the organization and requirements; this clause does not necessarily require two separate people for every case.
Do not confuse inspection results with valid authorization, or a signed release with evidence of conformity.
Do not release to the customer before planned arrangements are satisfactorily completed, unless approved by a relevant authority and, as applicable, the customer.
An exception is not a decision any employee can make because of deadline pressure.
Identify what remains incomplete, who may approve and whether customer approval applies.
This provision is not permission to bypass applicable statutory or regulatory requirements.
Make documented information available as evidence of product and service release.
Include evidence of conformity to acceptance criteria and traceability to people authorizing release.
Link the intended report version with verification results and authorization, showing what was released, on what basis and by whom.
Forms, electronic systems or linked records are possible methods; one format is not prescribed for everyone.
The attendance review is incomplete, so complete required arrangements before normal release.
After verifying data and meeting acceptance criteria, obtain authorized release and make evidence available.
Apply 8.7 where a discrepancy makes the output nonconforming.
Do not fabricate a review result simply to complete a required field.
Choose the latest product or service released to a customer.
Retrieve acceptance criteria and verification results and trace the authorizer and output covered by the authorization.
For an exception, review applicable approvals rather than relying on customer urgency.
Connect this with documented-information control under 7.5 and identification and status under 8.5.2 where applicable.
Controlled release rests on suitable verification arrangements, met acceptance criteria, traceable authorization and available evidence.
Exceptions require applicable approvals; deadline pressure should not make them the normal operating method.
