Clause 5.1 covers commitment to QMS effectiveness and commitment to customer focus.
Top management sets direction, integrates quality into the business and enables results.
It also ensures customer and applicable statutory and regulatory requirements are understood and consistently met, maintaining focus on enhanced satisfaction.
Top management may delegate tasks while retaining accountability for QMS effectiveness.
The quality function supports coordination and analysis but cannot replace leadership decisions on resources and conflicting objectives.
Commitment is evidenced by implemented decisions, reviewed results and removed barriers, not attendance alone.
If the factory aims to be a dependable office-furniture supplier, output volume alone is not enough.
Ensure the quality policy and objectives fit the organization’s context and strategic direction, balancing conformity, delivery and customer experience.
