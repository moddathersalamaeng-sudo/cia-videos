On a fit-out project, a skilled installer is asked to close a wall before the concealed-work inspection required by the project plan is complete.
Proceeding without raising the issue may hide a defect, leading to opening the wall, rework and customer delay.
Skill is present; awareness appears in understanding the impact and acting within authority, rather than equating task speed with overall success.
In the supplied 2026 edition, people working under organizational control must be aware of the quality policy and their contribution to QMS effectiveness and improved performance.
Awareness also covers nonconformity implications, relevant quality objectives, and organizational quality culture and ethical behaviour.
Explain these connected topics through daily tasks, instead of asking people to memorize phrases they cannot apply.
Competence concerns ability to perform; awareness helps people understand their contribution and the quality implications of their choices.
An installer may be skilled yet need to understand why required inspection precedes covering work, and who authorizes continuation.
The two complement each other: awareness messaging cannot replace missing skill, and a skill certificate cannot replace understanding responsibility and impact.
When the policy commits to meeting customer requirements, connect it to a site behaviour such as respecting the agreed inspection point.
Explain that a finished wall does not prove concealed-work conformity; clarity about inspection status protects the outcome.
Posting the policy is insufficient: discuss how it applies to a work situation and check understanding, without requiring word-for-word recall.
The installer contributes beyond the task itself: communicating inspection status helps the next team proceed on a sound basis.
Finding a defect before covering work reduces the risk of demolition, wasted materials and delay, illustrating the benefit of improved performance.
Use a realistic example and recognize useful reporting, so people understand that raising a concern supports the work.
If the project objective is to reduce finishing rework, explain the installer’s role, such as checking required inspection status before closing the designated area.
People need awareness of objectives relevant to their work and their contribution, rather than every organizational objective.
Ensure speed targets do not reward hiding defects; balance quality and time, and do not automatically treat more honest reports as failure.
Explain consequences clearly: covering uninspected work may affect conformity, the customer and downstream teams.
Falsely recording an inspection as complete also undermines the reliability of information used to authorize further work.
The aim is understanding responsibility and preventing recurrence, not threatening people in ways that encourage concealment.
Quality culture becomes visible under deadline pressure, when teams choose between transparency and a false record.
Explain ethical behaviour through honest work and inspection status, not concealing nonconformity, and using known reporting channels.
Management must reinforce the message in practice rather than pressuring people to bypass controls or penalizing honest reporting.
In the wall scenario, the installer checks status and informs the supervisor that the area has not met the required project-plan inspection point.
Maintain the appropriate condition within authority and ask the authorized person to arrange inspection and decide continuation under the controls.
This is an example response, not a requirement for every employee to stop an entire project whenever any concern arises.
Use a brief work example with a picture or model when helpful, in a language people understand, including temporary personnel under organizational control.
Ask people to explain the consequence, reporting route and action within authority, then observe application at work.
Attendance proves participation only; clause 7.3 does not prescribe an annual test or a specific awareness-record form. Choose suitable verification.
Policy and objectives set direction, competence provides ability, and communication defines the message, audience, timing and method.
When nonconformity appears, awareness supports understanding its impact and following controls rather than hiding it or passing it downstream.
Review messages when work changes or misunderstandings recur; use observations to improve the system rather than blaming every error on individual awareness.
Choose a meaningful work situation, connect policy, objectives, contribution, consequences and ethical behaviour, then check explanation and actual action.
